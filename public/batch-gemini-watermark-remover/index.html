Good batch

Original files from one workflow

  • Visible Gemini sparkle is still intact.
  • Images were not resized, cropped, or recompressed.
  • You own the files or have permission to edit them.

Review carefully

Screenshots and edited copies

  • Scaling can move the mark away from its expected position.
  • Compression can leave a faint edge around the restored area.
  • Hidden SynthID is outside the tool's scope.

Processing is local after the page and required assets have loaded. Keep the tab open until downloads finish.

Why was one file skipped?

The watermark may be cropped, resized, compressed, or unsupported. See troubleshooting.
