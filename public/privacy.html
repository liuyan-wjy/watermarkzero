Privacy

Privacy policy

Gemini Watermark Remover is designed as a local browser tool. The product goal is to let you remove the visible Gemini watermark without uploading the image to a remote processing service.

What we process

When you use the tool, the image file is processed in your browser using JavaScript and browser image APIs on your device. The site does not require an account or server upload to perform the Gemini watermark removal itself.

What we do not store

  • We do not intentionally store your uploaded images on a remote server for tool processing.
  • We do not require a user account to use the MVP.
  • We do not ask for payment details to use the online Gemini watermark remover.

Analytics and operational data

Like most public websites, the site may use standard infrastructure logs or privacy-friendly analytics to understand page performance and basic usage trends. That data should be configured to avoid collecting image content itself.

Contact and updates

If the product scope changes and server-side features are added later, this privacy page should be updated to reflect the new data flows before those changes go live.
