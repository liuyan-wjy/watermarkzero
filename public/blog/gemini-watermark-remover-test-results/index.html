How these tests are evaluated

The goal is not to claim that every Gemini image will work. The goal is to describe the conditions that make visible watermark cleanup reliable. For each sample, the original export is opened in the browser, processed locally, and then compared against the source at normal size and at a closer crop around the lower-right corner.

A result is treated as useful when the marked area no longer draws attention, the background texture still feels continuous, and the image does not show a square patch where the watermark used to be. A result is treated as questionable when a star outline, bright edge, blocky compression, or cloudy blur remains visible after processing.

This is also why the tool may skip some files. A skipped file can be a better outcome than a forced edit, especially when the image is for a product page, portfolio, listing, or marketing asset where corner artifacts are easy to notice.

What we measure before calling a result good

  • Detection confidence: the visible Gemini watermark should be found in the expected lower-right region.
  • Edge cleanliness: the star edges should not leave a bright outline or a smudged square.
  • Texture continuity: fabric, foliage, table grain, and soft gradients should remain believable after restoration.
  • Fail-safe behavior: unsupported images should be skipped rather than aggressively edited.

Unsupported cases are part of the test

A useful test page should include failure cases, not only attractive examples. The most common weak inputs are screenshots, social-media downloads, heavily compressed JPG files, and images where the lower-right corner has already been cropped. Those files may still look like Gemini images to a person, but the visible mark may no longer match the expected scale and margin.

If the remover cannot find the mark confidently, the safer behavior is to stop. That protects the original image from an obvious corner repair. For users, the practical fix is simple: try the original Gemini export first, avoid editing the corner before cleanup, and compare the preview at full size before downloading the final file.
