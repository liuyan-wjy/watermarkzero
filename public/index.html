Algorithm note

Reverse alpha restoration, not generic AI repainting

Gemini applies a semi-transparent visible logo in the lower-right corner. This site estimates the watermark position from known Gemini output patterns, reconstructs the alpha map, and then solves the reverse blending equation to recover the underlying pixels where possible.

FAQ

Questions about this Gemini watermark remover

Can I remove the Gemini watermark online without uploading my image?

Yes. This gemini watermark remover runs locally in your browser. The image stays on-device, so you can remove the visible Gemini watermark online without sending the file to a remote processing server.

Does this gemini logo remover work on every image?

No. It is designed for supported Gemini-generated images that contain the visible lower-right watermark pattern. If detection is not confident, the tool will skip removal instead of making destructive edits.

What is the difference between remove Gemini watermark and hidden watermark removal?

This site focuses on the visible Gemini watermark only. It does not remove hidden watermarking systems such as SynthID and should not be described as a universal hidden-watermark remover.

Is batch processing supported?

Yes. You can drag multiple images into the uploader, let the queue process them locally, and then download the results together as a ZIP file.
